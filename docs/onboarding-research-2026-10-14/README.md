# Onboarding research: Astra AI (2026-10-14)

Research for Adomeh's own onboarding. These are screenshots of the Astra AI sign-up flow (app.astra-ai.co), taken in one run on 2026-10-14 with an "Edeneum" test account. They are reference only, not a spec. The same product's Human Body Atlas is audited in `../atlas-audit-2026-10-14/`.

Files are in `screens/`, numbered in the order they were taken.

## Patterns worth taking

- **The quiz is the product pitch.** Every few questions there's a proof screen (07–08, 10, 13, 19). It turns an earlier answer into a promise ("your 10 min", "up to 2 grades").
- **Answers come back to you.** The study goal sets the daily goal on the streak screen (23). Country sets the curriculum. Interests theme the content. The profile card (18) shows all of it back.
- **Personal from the first screen.** The name is in the greeting, a tutor voice is chosen up front, and the home screen has one obvious next step.
- **Reasons to come back.** Streak, stars, missions, badges, daily puzzles and leaderboards.
- **Not for Adomeh:** fake urgency (discount countdowns), and grade-improvement claims we can't back up.
