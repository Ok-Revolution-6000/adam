# Draft: licensing request to Brill

**Send only after the Bos text is behind the invite-only tier**, so the letter and the site agree.

**To:** rights@degruyterbrill.com
**Subject:** Licence request: *The Medical Works of Moses Maimonides* (tr. Gerrit Bos, Brill 2021), display in a study platform

Dear Rights & Permissions team,

I run Adomeh (adomeh.com), a reading platform that sets classical medical texts beside an interactive 3D atlas of the human body. When a reader opens a chapter, each anatomical term is linked to the structure it names, with the modern description beside the classical one. Maimonides is our flagship author.

We would like to license the nine medical works in Gerrit Bos's 2021 English edition (Asthma, Poisons, Hemorrhoids, Rules, Medical Aphorisms, Coitus, Regimen of Health, Elucidation of Some Symptoms, Commentary on Hippocrates' Aphorisms) for display in our reader:

- **Display only.** No downloads, printing or copying features; text shown as a signed-in reader, chapter by chapter as in the book.
- **Text unaltered.** The only addition is a link on each anatomical term; the words and their order are not changed, and Brill's and Prof. Bos's names and copyright notice appear with every chapter.
- **Access.** Free with an account, with a visible link from every chapter to Brill's Maimonides' Medical Works Online for the critical apparatus and Arabic.
- **Fee.** We are open to a flat annual fee or a royalty and would like to hear what Brill prefers. We can share usage figures.

A working private demo is available; I can send a login so you can see the text in context before deciding.

Could you tell me whether this is something Brill would consider, who should handle it, and whether Prof. Bos's consent is also needed?

With thanks,
